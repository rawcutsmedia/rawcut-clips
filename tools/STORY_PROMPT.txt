HOW TO WRITE A RAWCUT SCARY STORY (read this every time before writing)

Goal: an ORIGINAL first-person scary story that keeps a stranger watching to the last line of a
65-95 second vertical video. Narrated by a slow, deep voice over Minecraft parkour.

OUTPUT: one JSON file per story:
  {"title": "...", "story": "...", "premise": "one line, for the repeat log", "theme": "..."}
  Paragraphs in "story" are separated by a blank line (\n\n). Plain text only.

LENGTH
- story: 170-230 words. Title + story together must narrate in 65-95 seconds. TikTok only pays for
  videos over 1 minute, so never go under 170 words.
- title: 10-22 words, written like a confession post: first person, ordinary setting, one detail
  that is wrong. It's read aloud first, so it is the hook.
  Good: "My smart doorbell keeps recording someone knocking at 3 AM. The footage shows an empty porch."
  Good: "I took a night job watching an empty parking garage. Rule 4 says never count the cars."
  Bad: "A scary story about a haunted house" (vague), anything that gives away the twist.

STRUCTURE
1. Line 1 restates the hook as a concrete, specific moment (a time, a place, an object).
2. Three escalating beats. Each one makes the wrong detail worse. Keep sentences short.
3. A reveal that reframes everything, set up by an early detail the listener half-forgot.
4. End on a short final line, 2-6 words, alone in its own paragraph. Leave the reader with the
   threat still in the room. No moral, no explanation, no "and that's why I never...".

STYLE
- Ordinary, believable life: apartments, night shifts, babysitting the house (not kids in danger),
  road trips, hiking, smart homes, voicemails, roommates, neighbors, hotel jobs, group chats.
- Concrete details beat adjectives ("the porch light was off, and I always leave it on" beats
  "it was terrifying"). Show, don't tell. No "suddenly", no "little did I know".
- Write for the ear: times like "3 AM" or "11:40" are fine; avoid symbols, emojis, abbreviations,
  ALL CAPS, parentheses, and long lists. Use dialogue sparingly, in quotes.
- Creepy and tense, never graphic. PG-13: dread, wrongness, presence. No gore, no injuries in
  detail, no self-harm or suicide, no sexual content, no children being hurt, no animals hurt,
  no real crimes or real tragedies, no real people, brands or real named places, no slurs,
  nothing hateful about any group.

IT IS FICTION
- Never copy or retell a real Reddit post, a creepypasta, a film or a book. Invent it.
- Never claim it's true, never say it was posted on Reddit, never name a subreddit or a real user.

VARIETY (check stories/log.jsonl first)
- Don't reuse a premise, monster type or twist from the last 60 entries.
- Rotate themes: rules-for-a-job, home intrusion that isn't a person, wrong number or voicemail,
  smart device/camera, neighbor, road or hiking, hotel or night shift, mirror or photo,
  sleep or memory, roommate or family member who isn't quite right.
- If insights.md says some themes or title styles get more views, write more of those.
