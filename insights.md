# What's working (early notes, Oct 20–21, 2026; replaced every Sunday once there's more data)

- Bold claims win: "Elon bought this $50K BOXABL" got 310 TikTok views, "built in under 1 hour" 129,
  "car dropped on the roof" 90, and the "a home every 15 minutes" goal was the user's best performer.
- Plain feature tours flop: the shower clip got 0 views, ceilings 9.
- Rules for now:
  1. Pick moments with a big number or a bold claim: speed, price, durability, Elon, "fix housing".
  2. Hook titles and caption line 1 state that claim flatly, or ask "Would you…?" / "Is this the
     future of…?". No descriptive titles.
  3. Keep it positive toward the brand. Debate, not negativity.
- YouTube Shorts are getting almost no views so far, so judge hooks by TikTok for now.
