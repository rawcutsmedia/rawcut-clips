# What's working (updated by the evening job; GTA section daily once there's data)

## GTA clips (Clix GTA VI marathon on @mediarawcut, started Nov 18, 2026)
- Night 1 (Nov 18, only 2 clips, so an early signal): ~46 views each; viewers left after ~5 seconds
  (18% of a 29 s clip, 26% of an 18 s clip). Until there's more data: the first second must show
  the action itself, and keep clips 10-20 s. Both hooks were plain statements; try questions/reactions.
- Starting rules from research until the numbers say otherwise:
  1. Open on the peak moment within the first 2 seconds; cut to 10-30 seconds.
  2. Funny moments, fails and wild chases first; skip menus, long talking and anything negative about Clix.
  3. Hook names Clix and is a question or a reaction ("Clix did NOT expect this").
  4. One experiment a night: vary one thing (hook style, length, moment) and record it.
  5. Captions (user's call, Nov 20): short GTA 6 > GTA 5 hot takes naming Clix, to drive comments.
     The 4 posts before that used longer descriptive captions; compare `caption_style` once measured.
